GlobalCorp Employee Benefits and Perks Guide (Effective March 2026)

1. Health Insurance Coverage
GlobalCorp offers two primary medical plans through HealthTrust: the Premier PPO and the Value HDHP. 
- Premier PPO: Features a $500 individual deductible, 90% co-insurance coverage for in-network providers, and a $25 copay for primary care visits. The monthly employee premium contribution is $180 for individual coverage.
- Value HDHP: Features a $3,000 individual deductible, 80% co-insurance coverage after the deductible is met, and a health savings account (HSA) with a company match of up to $750 annually. The monthly employee premium contribution is $45 for individual coverage.

2. Parental Leave Policy
Full-time employees who have been with GlobalCorp for at least 12 continuous months are eligible for up to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. This leave can be taken continuously or split into two separate blocks within the first year of the child's arrival.

3. Professional Development Stipend
Each full-time employee is allocated an annual professional development budget of $1,500. This fund can be used for conferences, online certification courses, technical books, and professional memberships. Unused funds do not roll over into the next calendar year and expire on December 31st.
